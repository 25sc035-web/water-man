The optimizer solves the blend equation to give treated water first to high-tolerance stages (Washing first, then Bleaching, then Dyeing) while calculating whether each stage is limited by quality or supply.

The Key Insight: Quality Constraints (TDS & Hardness), Not Just Volume

3:00 - 4:00 (1 min)

Talking Script: "Here is our core engineering innovation: in real textile mills, water reuse is limited by chemistry, NOT just hydraulic volume. High TDS precipitates dyes and causes shade variation, while hardness causes calcium soap scaling. The optimizer uses the blend conservation equation:"

C_blend = (V_fresh · C_fresh + V_treated · C_treated) / V_demand

"Notice how the algorithm gives treated water first to Washing & Rinsing (which tolerates 700 mg/L TDS). All 7,450 L goes to washing, yielding a safe blended TDS of 523 mg/L. Dyeing is flagged as limitedBy: Quality, while Bleaching is flagged as limitedBy: Supply—directly telling the engineer where to invest in RO capacity!"

Engineering Resolution & System Verification Details

1. Water Savings Equation (18.625%)

Reduction = (7,450 L/d ÷ 40,000 L/d) × 100% = 18.625%

Mathematically verified and tracked consistently across the entire dashboard and report.

2. Cost Savings Parametric Formula (₹9,685)

Savings = 7,450 L/d × 26 d × (₹50 ÷ 1,000 L) = ₹9,685/mo

Dynamic variables: avoided freshwater, working days/mo, and water tariff are editable in Step 3.

3. Complete Mass Balance Accounting

Inflows = Outflows: 32,550 L/d Fresh + 7,450 L/d Reclaimed = 40,000 L/d Demand. Effluent (25,100 L/d) balances across tertiary reuse (7,450 L/d), sludge/evaporation (16,200 L/d), and discharge (1,450 L/d).

4. Dynamic Quality-Bounded Recommendations

Process savings are dynamically computed by allocateTreatedWater() using stage TDS limits (Washing: 700 mg/L) and hardness thresholds, reporting whether limited by quality or supply.

5. Dynamic Payback Period Calculation

Payback = CapEx (₹) ÷ Net Monthly Savings (₹)

Phase 1 automation (₹35,000 CapEx) delivers an exact 3.61-month payback against net monthly savings.

6. End-to-End Functionality Verification

All interactive controls—batch sliders, shade depth multipliers, Chart.js instances, PDF generation, JSON state export, and 3 scenario toggles—are fully verified.
